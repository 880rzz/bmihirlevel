EVENT RECAP

When the Autumn Fairy came to visit

On Saturday, the Autumn Fairy brought a wealth of seasonal treasures to the Vienna Hungarian School. Children pressed fresh juice from autumn fruit, carved pumpkin lanterns, made door decorations from natural materials and settled into the little gnome house to play with its tiny tea sets.

The BüféMi team prepared homemade cakes and Hungarian flatbread for children and adults alike. Our face painter was busy throughout the day, with children queuing for autumn-inspired designs.

Amid the lively activity, there was also room for quiet: reflection, stillness and turning inward. Thank you for celebrating with us!

Our next celebration will take place on 13 December at the Vienna Hungarian School, as we prepare together for Advent.

More than a school

Let's build a real school community

We want Bécsi Magyar Iskola to be about more than classes: a place where families get to know one another, make connections and organise activities together.

BMI Parents' Community

Let's meet beyond the classroom

We're launching the Bécsi Magyar Iskola Parents' Community. The idea is simple: a place where parents can connect, talk, exchange ideas and arrange activities together.

We'd like to organise weekend trips, museum visits, skiing days, family get-togethers and other shared activities.

We don't want to plan everything ourselves: We welcome ideas and suggestions from parents.
